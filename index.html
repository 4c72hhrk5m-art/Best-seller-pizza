Welcome to The CABS Foodtrip

Craving your favorite local foods?

Check out the CABS Food Park!

Located near Cabuyao Athletes Basic School and directly behind Pamantasan ng Cabuyao, CABS Food Park is an open-air food hub and popular night market for students, families, and locals. The vibrant venue features an array of independent stalls and tiangge booths serving everything from classic Filipino street food and grilled BBQ to seafood scallops, milk tea, and fresh fruit drinks. Designed around communal outdoor seating illuminated by festive string lights, it offers an energetic yet casual setting perfect for late-night food crawls, study breaks, and informal gatherings.

Driven by its close proximity to surrounding schools and universities, the food park is widely known for its budget-friendly pricing and lively community atmosphere. Beyond everyday dining, the space frequently transforms into a festive gathering spot hosting seasonal holiday light displays, local bazaars, and occasional live music performances. Its combination of diverse culinary choices, affordable prices, and warm open-air ambiance makes CABS Food Park a go-to destination for authentic local nightlife and casual dining in Cabuyao City, Laguna.

About Us

Meet the Team Behind The CABS Foodtrip

We are a group of students who created The CABS Foodtrip as a website project designed to introduce and showcase the different food choices available at CABS Food Park in Cabuyao, Laguna.

Our goal is to make it easier for students, families, and visitors to discover different food stalls, learn about the food they offer, find affordable options, and enjoy a convenient food-trip experience in one place.

Through this website, we want to share our interest in food while also practicing our skills in web design, HTML, CSS, and teamwork. Each member contributed to the planning and development of The CABS Foodtrip.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What types of food are available in the area?

A: There are different types of food available, including Filipino dishes, snacks, desserts, and drinks.

Q: What are the most popular foods to try?

A: Some of the most popular foods to try include the best-selling and most affordable dishes and snacks offered by the stores in the area.

Q: Are there affordable food options available?

A: Yes! There are affordable food options available for customers looking for delicious meals and snacks at reasonable prices. Among them, the most affordable one is street foods.

Q: What foods are good for students or people on a budget?

A: Budget-friendly shawarma meals, street food snacks, and refreshing drinks are available for students and customers who want affordable options.

Q: Are there desserts and drinks available?

A: Yes! Many stores offer a variety of desserts and refreshing drinks to enjoy.

Q: Are there Filipino food options?

A: Yes! Several stores serve Filipino dishes and local favorites.

Q: Which food is best for sharing with friends?

A: Foods with larger servings or combo meals are great choices for sharing with friends and family. Some examples of these are pizza and there are also seafood group meal options.

Q: What are the operating hours of the stores?

A: The general oprating hours is 4 PM to 9 PM. However, they also vary depending on the store, so it is best to check each store's information.

Q: Which stores offer the best value for money?

A: The stores that has comdo meals in their menu usually provides affordable prices, good-quality food, and generous portions that maximizes the value of your money.
